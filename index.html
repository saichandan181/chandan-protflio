01 / About

Engineer by training, automator by instinct.

Who I am

I’m Sanda Reddy Sai Chandan — a computer science engineer specialising in AI & ML. These days I split my time between shipping cross-platform mobile apps with React Native + Expo and building generative-AI systems: LLM apps, multimodal vision tools and multi-agent pipelines that plan, act and verify their own work.

I also do Salesforce platform engineering — Agentforce, Apex, Flow and the Tooling API — and full-stack web with React, Next.js, Node and Python. I care about software that is fast, honest about what it does, and usable by everyone.
